Privacy policy

yt-ai-pipeline · last updated 27 September 2026

yt-ai-pipeline is a personal tool that the owner of the YouTube channel @dogevideos-vid runs on their own computer to manage that channel. It has no users other than the channel owner.

YouTube API Services

yt-ai-pipeline uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data it accesses

  • Basic information about the owner's YouTube channel (name, handle, subscriber and video counts).
  • The channel's videos and their public statistics (views, likes, comments).
  • YouTube Analytics reports of the channel (watch time, traffic sources, audience) - read only.
  • Comments on the channel's videos, so the owner can read, reply to and moderate them.
  • Permission to upload videos to the owner's own channel and to set their title, description, tags and release time.

How the data is used

Only to upload and schedule the owner's own videos on the owner's own channel and to show the channel's statistics, analytics and comments to the owner inside the tool. Nothing is done with the data for anyone else, and it is not used for advertising.

Storage and sharing

All data, including the sign-in token, is stored only on the owner's own computer. It is not sent to any server other than Google's own APIs, and it is not sold or shared with third parties.

Retention and deletion

Statistics are kept locally for as long as the owner uses the tool and can be deleted at any time by deleting the tool's data folder. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; the tool then stops accessing the account.
