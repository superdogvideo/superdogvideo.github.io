yt-ai-pipeline

yt-ai-pipeline is a personal tool of the YouTube channel @dogevideos-vid. It runs on the channel owner's own computer and is used only by the channel owner.

With the owner's permission it uploads the owner's own gameplay clips to the owner's own YouTube channel, schedules their release, and shows the channel's statistics, analytics and comments to the owner. It is not a public service and has no other users.
